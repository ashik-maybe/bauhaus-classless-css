Issue No. 03 — The Modernist Series

form follows function.

A strictly semantic framework rooted in the principles of the Bauhaus movement. By removing the unnecessary, we find the essential. No shadows, no gradients, no classes—just pure structure.

01. the typography

In Swiss design, typography is the architecture. We use the geometric Inter for bold, structural headers and the classic Libre Baskerville for long-form narrative. This contrast ensures that information is both beautiful and readable.

"The ultimate aim of all creative activity is a building!" — Walter Gropius

02. visual geometry

Images are not merely decorations; they are structural components. By default, all media is rendered in high-contrast grayscale to maintain the print aesthetic, returning to color only upon user interaction.

03. systemic data

Data tables follow the logic of a blueprint. Heavy horizontal strokes create a clear hierarchy, while hover states invert the ink and paper to highlight the user's path.

04. structural forms

Inputs are stripped of their digital boxes, appearing as simple architectural lines. This reduces visual noise and focuses the user on the act of communication.
